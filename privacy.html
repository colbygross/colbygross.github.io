Athena Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What Athena is

Athena is a personal, self-hosted application. Each copy runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by that owner. There is no Athena server, service or company that receives your data.

Google user data Athena accesses

When you connect Google Calendar, Athena requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope. It uses it to:

  • read events from your primary Google Calendar so they appear in Athena;
  • create, update and delete events in your Google Calendar when you do so in Athena.

How the data is stored

Calendar events and the OAuth token are stored only on the computer running your copy of Athena, in its local database and storage folder. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Sharing

Athena does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Athena's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

You can revoke Athena's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file and Athena's local database removes all stored data.
